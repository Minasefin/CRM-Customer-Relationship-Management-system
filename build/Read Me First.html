Opening Contractor CRM for the first time

Your computer will block the app once, because it isn't from the App Store or Microsoft Store. That's expected. It only takes a minute, and you only do it once.

On a Mac

  1. Drag Contractor CRM into your Applications folder, if you haven't already.
  2. Open Applications and double-click Contractor CRM. Your Mac says it can't verify the app. Click Done.

    Don't click Move to Trash. If you did, drag the app from the installer into Applications again.

  3. Open Privacy & Security:
    Open Privacy & Security

    If your browser asks to open System Settings, click Allow. Or open it yourself: Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security.

  4. Scroll to the bottom, to Security. Next to "Contractor CRM was blocked", click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again.
  5. That's it. From now on it opens like any other app. The first time, Contractor CRM asks you to create its own password (separate from your Mac password). There's no reset, so keep it somewhere safe.
No "Open Anyway" button?

Some Mac versions don't show it. Open Terminal (press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Return), paste this line, and press Return. Then open the app normally.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Contractor CRM.app"

This only removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag from this one app.

On Windows

  1. Double-click Contractor CRM Setup.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The app installs and opens. Find it later in the Start menu.

Why this happens

Apple and Microsoft show this warning for any app from a developer who hasn't paid for a signing certificate. Contractor CRM works completely offline: it never connects to the internet, and everything you enter stays on this computer.
